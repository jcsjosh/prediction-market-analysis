Ground rules

The data edge is thin

Calibration edges are usually 1–3¢ per contract. Fees eat most of that on the taker side, which is why limit orders tend to rank higher.

Your view is the real edge

Enter your own win % when you know something the market doesn't. Pass --views views.csv to the scanner to rank those markets too.

Arbitrage goes first

Risk-free baskets are guaranteed by the contract rules, not by statistics. Check the fill size on every leg before sending.

Size small

Quarter-Kelly with a 5% cap per bet. Many bets that share a cause (same game, same CPI print) count as one bet.
